## Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
TerraSense UI for Environmental Intelligence. 
Visual Style: Dark mode, futuristic, premium glassmorphism. Deep dark green/teal backgrounds (#071316, #0C1E22). Cards use slightly lighter dark teal surfaces (#10262A) with subtle gradients or glows. Primary accents are bright neon green (#58E3A5) and cyan (#52BFF4). Typography is highly legible and modern (Inter and Space Grotesk). Minimalist, data-heavy, professional aesthetic suitable for a high-tech command center. Do not use plain grays or blues—always use the teal/green tinted neutrals. Provide distinct states for loading and errors.
Platform: Desktop web (Responsive).
